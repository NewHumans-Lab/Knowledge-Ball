# Knowledge Ball promotional film — claim audit

Source of truth: [Knowledge Ball White Paper 1.0 (English)](../public/whitepapers/Knowledge-Ball-White-Paper-EN.pdf) and its Chinese counterpart.

| Scene | Film wording category                 | White-paper-safe boundary                                                                                                                                          |
| ----- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 01–02 | Problem framing                       | Describes provenance and dependency-graph gaps; makes no product capability claim.                                                                                 |
| 03    | Product model                         | Uses stable `KnowledgeNode`, immutable `ClaimVersion`, explicit `ReasoningNode`, and non-destructive versioning.                                                   |
| 04    | Committed design                      | Labels the three semantic shells and deterministic spatial-memory goal as design, not proven scale.                                                                |
| 05    | Committed protocol                    | Creation is distinct from acceptance; verdicts are version-bound, bounded, revisable, and not popularity-made truth.                                               |
| 06    | Committed protocol                    | Energy is internal accounting with version-bound stakes and net-zero double entry; explicitly not a token launch or external financial value.                      |
| 07    | Hard boundary                         | Public validity and private mastery remain strictly separated.                                                                                                     |
| 08    | Working prototype + backend direction | TypeScript/Vite/Three.js and shared packaging are described as implemented; Supabase/Postgres is described as direction; models propose while the protocol admits. |
| 09    | Validation gates                      | Theory is coherent; engineering viability is moderate; commercial value and internet-scale operation remain unproven.                                              |

## Excluded claims

- No claim of full decentralization.
- No blockchain, cryptocurrency, or token-economics framing.
- No claim that the system guarantees eternal truth.
- No claim that the visualization replaces accountable review.
- No claim of production-grade internet-scale validation.
